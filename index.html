Field notes

A small page about birds seen on the morning walk.

  • Oriental magpie-robin

    Black and white, tail cocked high. Sings from rooftops before sunrise.

  • Javan myna

    Dark grey with a yellow bill and legs. Walks the grass verges in noisy groups.

  • Yellow-vented bulbul

    Brown with a bright yellow patch under the tail. A cheerful, bubbling call.

  • Olive-backed sunbird

    Tiny and quick. The male shows an iridescent blue-black throat at the hibiscus.

  • Spotted dove

    Pinkish-brown with a speckled collar. Soft, repeated cooing from the trees.

  • Collared kingfisher

    Turquoise back and a white collar. Perches on lamp posts by the canal.
